# Getting started

1. Ask your manager to request access. Orders Hub access is approved by the finance team.
2. Sign in with your company account.
3. Pick your region in the top-right menu. The orders hub remembers this choice.
4. Open **Orders** to see today's orders, or **Summary** for monthly totals.

If the page is empty, check that a region is selected. Orders Hub shows nothing until you pick one.
