# Frequently asked questions

**Can I see orders from other regions?**
Only if your role allows it. Ask in #orders-hub-help.

**Why do monthly totals change after the month ends?**
Late cancellations are applied for five working days. After that the month is closed in Order Desk.

**Is there a mobile app?**
No. Order Desk works in the browser on a phone, but the export button is desktop only.
