# Getting started

1. Ask your manager to request access. Order Desk access is approved by the finance team.
2. Sign in with your company account.
3. Pick your region in the top-right menu. Order Desk remembers this choice.
4. Open **Orders** to see today's orders, or **Summary** for monthly totals.

If the page is empty, check that a region is selected. Order Desk shows nothing until you pick one.
