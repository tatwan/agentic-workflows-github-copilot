# Team inbox

Files a team member saved from email and chat over a few weeks, all in one folder.

| Folder | What it holds |
|---|---|
| `inbox/` | Everything that was saved, not sorted yet: notes, spreadsheets (CSV), images and PDFs |

The team works on two projects: an orders dashboard for regional leads, and the Q2 release of the orders system.
A few files are office admin, and a few are personal.

The team, its projects and its files are fictional.
